# PA × Vanguard Licence to Operate — review edition

## Scope and assumptions
PA means PA Consulting; Vanguard means the investment management organisation. No engagement-specific SOW, policy pack, internal contacts or approved training requirements were supplied. This is a functional review edition, not an official client policy or learning management system.

## Adaptation from the reference
- Retained: organisational context, scope and responsibilities, delivery standards, secure working, risk escalation, evidence and handover.
- Reframed around the client: investor outcomes, joint PA/client delivery, information handling and role-based pathways.
- Removed: GDS and civil-service governance, GOV.UK products, GovAssure, government-specific frameworks and mandatory specialist SOC/SIEM/SOAR assumptions.
- Combined specialist technical guidance into an optional module required for technology, data and assurance roles. Added governance focus for delivery and assurance roles.
- Replaced the reference's demo-user management dashboard with an individual learning record. No fabricated completion, personnel, policy owners or client integrations.

## Behaviour
10 modules; 8 core modules; role-based additions; 3 reading sections and 1 knowledge check per module; completion requires a correct check; optional saved modules and notes; a 5-scenario final assessment with 80% pass threshold unlocked after the required pathway; device-local state; text downloads for playbook and learning record. A role change retains module completion and resets the assessment. Completion does not grant access or client approval.

## Owner review required before operational adoption
Confirm audience, contract and service boundaries, policy links and versions, incident routes, mandatory training, role mapping, assessment expectations, evidence retention, accessibility requirements and identity/learning-system integration. The proposed timings are estimates, not measured training durations.
